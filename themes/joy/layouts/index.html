Why joy?

Joy is the emotion advertising promises most often and explains least. These essays slow down a single campaign at a time and ask what actually produced the feeling: a conditioned cue, a moment of surprise, a story that pulled the viewer in, the pleasure of being seen.

Each one ends with the research it draws on, so the argument can be checked. The archive lists all of them by date and topics groups them by theme.
